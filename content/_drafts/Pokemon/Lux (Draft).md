### Personality:
Lux is a naive and emotionally transparent Shinx, prone to showing his feelings openly regardless of the consequences. Though he initially presents as energetic and extroverted, this is largely a front for a shy and easily frightened nature. His talkative demeanor masks his social anxiety. He is highly trusting and often credulous, easily believing what he is told.

When faced with danger, Lux tends to freeze and struggles to break out of it. He has a tendency to cling to those he trusts for any difficult situation, often struggling to act without support. Despite this, he is far more capable when he has someone to rely on.

He is genuinely kind and self-sacrificing, and when someone he cares for is in need, he will push himself far beyond his limits to support them. Loyal to a fault, his greatest strength is often his greatest vulnerability: he gives himself away too freely.

### Backstory:
##### Early childhood
Lux was born and raised in a small town in the foothills of the Grass Continent, far from the capital and even further from the place of legends, Treasure Town. Despite its size, the town sat along a common trade route and was a frequent stop for travelers and skyfarers passing between the coast and the interior.

His mother ran the local tavern, and Lux grew up surrounded by travelers and their stories. Many spoke of passing rumors, hidden caves, and treasures yet uncovered. Helping his mother at the tavern, he overheard a great deal of this talk and became fixated on it, often daydreaming about what it would be like to take on such adventures himself.

##### Growing up
The other children in town did not share his interest. To them, Lux was a child obsessed with silly stories, and they teased him for it often. Lux made no effort to hide how much it bothered him, and the teasing continued. He eventually learned to keep his enthusiasm guarded in public, though he never stopped dreaming.

Lux's father was a merchant skyfarer and he taught Lux how to read stars for navigation. Lux took to stargazing as a way to pass time alone, spending many nights in the hills above town where the sky was clear and no one else was around. The stars became his refuge.

He kept an old book, given to him on his tenth birthday — a small curio his father found during one of his travels. It was a strange hand written encyclopedia from an unknown author, cataloguing stars and constellations. Lux later began filling its empty pages with his own findings, and it became his most cherished possession.

As he grew older, the teasing faded into indifference. He remained an outcast among the town's children, but he stayed kind and trusting, offering help to other townsfolk even when it was not in his interest.

##### Present
Lux wanted nothing more than to become an explorer himself: to discover hidden places, find new treasures, and uncover the secrets left behind from the era of heroes. In the years that followed, he prepared as best he could — memorizing maps, listening to travelers, and learning the stars well enough to believe he could navigate by them.

On the morning after his fourteenth birthday, with his parents' blessing, he left home.

He set out toward Treasure Town.
