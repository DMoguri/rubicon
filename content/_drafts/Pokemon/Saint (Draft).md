### Personality:
Saint is an aloof and emotionally distant Eevee, though he reads as indifferent rather than actively rude. He rarely engages with hostility or small talk; when confronted, he tends to go quiet and act unilaterally rather than argue. He is highly independent and over-functions by habit, preferring to handle problems himself rather than involve others, even when cooperation would be more efficient. His initiative is frequently mistaken for arrogance, though he does not view himself as superior.

Despite this exterior, Saint is observant and perceptive, with an exceptional ability to read situations and sense danger before it arrives. He is a skilled tactician, capable of coordinating multiple Pokemon effectively and understanding their limits in a crisis, yet he deliberately avoids teamwork, as he is unwilling to place others in harm's way. When he does choose to help, he does so without expecting anything in return.

In truth, Saint cares deeply for those around him, but he keeps his emotions carefully hidden behind a composed exterior. In lighter moments, he displays a big appetite, which contrasts humorously with his otherwise reserved demeanor.
